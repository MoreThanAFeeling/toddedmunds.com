About

Todd has spent 35+ years at the intersection of operational technology and IT: building packaging machine control systems, serving as a solution architect at Rockwell Automation, building an IoT business at a systems integrator, and helping build Cisco's industrial automation business.

At Dell Technologies he built the smart manufacturing business to more than $2B in annual revenue and now sets strategy for manufacturing infrastructure and physical AI, working across an alliance ecosystem that includes NVIDIA, Microsoft, Accenture, EY, Rockwell Automation and Siemens.

Speaking topics

Physical AI and the AI-native factory. Digital twins from simulation to decision. Edge computing as the foundation for industrial AI. Private 5G and connectivity for operations. Building the business case for scaled manufacturing AI.

Frequent speaker at Hannover Messe and NVIDIA GTC. Chairs the Digital Twin Consortium Manufacturing Workgroup. BSEE, Kansas State University; two US patents.
